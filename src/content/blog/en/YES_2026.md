From 9 to 12 September, the Faculty of Chemistry at Jagiellonian University hosted the ninth edition of the Young European Scientists Conference (YES2026), dedicated to functional polymeric materials. The event brought together more than 50 early-career researchers from 12 European countries.

During one of the conference sessions, MSc Eng. Michał Sroka, a member of the R\&D team of the LIDER XIV project, delivered a presentation entitled “Cationic star-like copolymers with a sucrose core as excipients in the development of solid sustained-release drug formulations.” His talk explored the use of atom transfer radical polymerization (ATRP) to synthesize cationic star-shaped polymers with a sucrose core, designed as pharmaceutical excipients for modified drug release in solid dosage forms, particularly sustained-release tablets.

Replacing commercially available excipients with the newly synthesized polymers may reduce tablet mass while maintaining the drug's pharmaceutical availability. This approach could improve patient convenience during medication administration and potentially lower manufacturing costs.

The research presented and participation in the conference were made possible through financial support from the National Centre for Research and Development (NCBR) under the LIDER XIV project.
