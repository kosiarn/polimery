As part of the LIDER XIV project, Prof. Dr. Izabela Zaborniak participated in ACS Fall 2026, organized by the American Chemical Society (August 23-27, 2026, Chicago, USA), where she delivered a lecture entitled “Design of branched polymeric excipients through controlled polymerization: Structure–property relationships in drug release.”

The presentation focused on research conducted within the LIDER XIV project, which aims to develop a new generation of branched polymeric pharmaceutical excipients for use in modified drug release systems.

Particular attention was given to the relationships between molecular structure, physicochemical properties, and polymer performance in drug release. 

Participation in ACS Fall 2026 also provided an opportunity to present the latest research findings to the international chemistry community, exchange expertise with researchers working in polymer chemistry, functional materials, and pharmaceutical technology, and establish new scientific collaborations.

The participation was funded within the LIDER XIV project, supported by the National Centre for Research and Development (NCBR), contract no. LIDER14/0058/2023.
